What is DentalGuide AI?

DentalGuide AI is an educational tool designed to help patients better understand common symptoms after dental treatments such as tooth extractions, root canals, fillings, aligners, and cleanings.

The system provides general guidance based on symptom type, severity, and time since treatment, helping users understand when symptoms may be normal and when professional dental evaluation may be needed.

DentalGuide AI provides general educational information only and does not replace professional dental diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical care.
